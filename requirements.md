# Project Requirements for Niche Shilajit E-Commerce Site

## Introduction
This requirements document outlines a plan to build a niche e-commerce site selling pure Himalayan Shilajit, inspired by goldenshilajit.pk. The goal is to create a trustworthy, educational, and seamless buying experience using a tech stack suitable for simple web hosting (e.g., Hostinger shared hosting). This document supports experimenting with different technology stacks.

---

## Business Goals
- Sell authentic Himalayan Shilajit resin online.
- Build customer trust with certifications and educational content.
- Grow sales locally and internationally.
- Deliver a fast, mobile-friendly experience.

---

## User Personas
- **Health-Conscious Shoppers**: Seeking trusted supplements, value certification and scientific backing.
- **Casual Buyers**: May have heard of Shilajit benefits, require simple info and reassurance.
- **Repeat Customers**: Looking for easy reordering and fast checkout.

---

## User Stories
- As a first-time visitor, I want to see clear proof of product authenticity so I feel safe buying online.
- As a customer, I want to read educational content and FAQs so I can learn about Shilajit’s benefits before purchasing.
- As a mobile shopper, I want fast page loads and easy navigation so buying is easy from my phone.
- As an international buyer, I want currency/payment options suitable for my country.
- As a returning customer, I want an easy way to reorder quickly.
- As a user, I want to contact support easily if I have a question.

---

## Pages & Features (Design Inspired by GoldenShilajit.pk)

### 1. Homepage
- Hero section with premium Shilajit imagery and call-to-action "Buy Now"
- Trust badges & certifications (Eurofins, BSCG, COAs)
- Customer testimonials/reviews slider
- Short intro about the company/product
- Quick links to lab results, benefits, and the shop

### 2. Shop/Product Listing Page
- Grid of products (even if just one SKU, shown attractively)
- Price, Add to Cart button
- Info on shipping, guarantees

### 3. Product Detail Page
- Large product image(s)
- Details/descriptions (origin, purity, suggested usage, benefits)
- Downloadable or viewable Certificates of Analysis (COAs)
- Money-back guarantee and trust signals
- Customer reviews
- "Add to Cart" prominently displayed

### 4. Checkout/Cart Page
- Minimalist, fast, mobile-first cart and checkout
- Options for local (JazzCash, EasyPaisa) and international (Stripe, PayPal) payments
- Trust and security badges throughout checkout
- Coupon/promo handling

### 5. Lab Tests & Certificates Page
- Gallery or PDF viewers of recent lab results and certifications
- Brief explanation of each certificate/unlock confidence
- Download options

### 6. Benefits & Science Page (Educational Blog/Knowledge Base)
- In-depth articles/blog posts: "What is Shilajit?", "How Shilajit is sourced?", "Health Benefits Explained"
- Author information or sources linked to studies
- Internal linking to shop

### 7. About Us Page
- Story of the brand
- Sourcing and quality assurance processes
- Imagery of source region (Himalayas, natural landscapes)

### 8. Contact Page
- Contact form
- WhatsApp link/button
- Email and phone info

### 9. Policy Pages
- Refund Policy (e.g., 45-day guarantee)
- Privacy Policy
- Shipping Policy
- Terms of Service

---

## Technical Constraints & Considerations
- Must run on simple shared hosting, e.g., Hostinger
- PHP and MySQL compatible (all standard WordPress/WooCommerce functionality)
- Mobile-first and optimized for speed
- Easy to extend with plugins, minor custom code

---

## Design Principles (Inspired by GoldenShilajit.pk)
- Warm, natural colors (browns, golds, whites)
- Clean, bold typography
- Large, trust-filled hero with product and lab certifications visible immediately
- Minimal navigation for ease on mobile
- Reassurance (badges, money-back guarantee) visible at each conversion step

---

## Content Requirements
- Product photography (high-res images, lifestyle shots)
- Lab certificates and test result images/PDFs
- Customer testimonials (photos, first names, locations)
- Blog content for SEO (planned topics)
  - Origin of Shilajit
  - Benefits for health
  - Scientific research summaries
- FAQs

---

## Analytics & Marketing Integration
- Google Analytics or alternative for visitor tracking
- Facebook Pixel (optional, for retargeting)
- Basic SEO on all content and blog
- Email subscription (optional, for future marketing)

---

## Future-Proofing
- Ability to add more products, variations, or bundles
- Local/international shipping and tax zones
- Multilingual support (optional for future expansion)
